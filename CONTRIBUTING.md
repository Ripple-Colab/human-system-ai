# Corrections

Every person, tie, event, release and value on the map links to a public
source. If you spot something wrong or missing:

1. Open an issue.
2. Say which item it is (the person, the tie, or the date and title of the event).
3. Give a source that supports the correction: a news report, a court filing,
   a company post or a Wikipedia article.

Corrections without a source cannot be checked, so they will wait. You can
also email mark@ripplecolab.com.
